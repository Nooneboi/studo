# Chee Skool Myanmar Explanation Style

Status: working standard for review-stage learner content.

## Goal

Myanmar support should feel like a capable tutor sitting beside the learner: close, clear, calm, and useful.

It is not a translation layer. It explains the reasoning.

## Voice

Use natural Myanmar sentence structure and keep GED terms in English when students are likely to meet those terms in lessons or on the test.

Good examples of retained terms:
- Main Idea
- Evidence
- Inference
- Claim
- Tone
- passage
- answer
- detail
- supporting detail
- whole passage

Prefer conversational connectors that sound normal in study talk:
- ဒီမှာ…
- ကြည့်ရင်…
- ဘာလို့လဲဆိုတော့…
- အဓိကက…
- လွယ်လွယ်စဉ်းစားရင်…
- နောက်တစ်ခါ ဒီလိုစစ်…

Avoid formal textbook Burmese such as:
- ဤ / ထို
- ဖြစ်ပါသည်
- ဖော်ပြထားခြင်းဖြစ်သည်
- ကိုယ်စားပြုသည်

Avoid over-casual or social-media language:
- bro
- ကွာ
- constant နော် / ရှင့်
- motivational filler
- excessive emoji

Usually avoid direct pronouns. Prefer:
- “ရွေးထားတဲ့ answer…”
instead of:
- “မင်းရွေးထားတဲ့ answer…”

## Structure

Use no more than three short sections.

For a wrong selected answer:
1. **ဒီ answer က ဘာကြောင့်မကိုက်တာလဲ**
2. **ဘာလို့ မှန်တဲ့ answer က ပိုကိုက်တာလဲ**
3. **နောက်တစ်ခါ ဒီလိုစစ်**

For a correct selected answer:
1. **ဘာလို့ ဒီ answer မှန်တာလဲ**
2. **Passage ထဲမှာ ဘာကိုကြည့်ရလဲ**
3. **နောက်တစ်ခါ ဒီလိုစစ်**

For concept / sorting / open-response support:
1. **ဒီမှာ ဘာစစ်နေတာလဲ**
2. **လွယ်လွယ်စဉ်းစားရင်**
3. **နောက်တစ်ခါ ဒီလိုစစ်**

Not every explanation must use all three sections. Remove a section if it adds no teaching value.

## Content rules

- Refer to the actual answer text or passage idea, never internal IDs such as s1, s2, i1.
- Explain why a tempting wrong answer is tempting when useful.
- Keep one explanation focused on one reasoning problem.
- Do not merely repeat the English feedback in Myanmar.
- Point back to the passage.
- End with one move the learner can reuse on the next question.
- Do not introduce claims that the source passage does not support.
- Keep explanations short enough to read on a phone.

## QA check

Before approving a Myanmar explanation, ask:
1. Is it accurate?
2. Does it sound natural when read aloud?
3. Would a GED learner understand it quickly?
4. Does it explain the reasoning rather than translate the English?
5. Does it avoid unnecessary formality and unnecessary slang?
6. Does it help with the next question?
