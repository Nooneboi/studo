## Source-derived decisions

- Exam-style RLA content is source-text based; no stand-alone exam items.
- Reading passage sets use 400–900 word texts and roughly 6–8 associated questions.
- Overall passage-bank target: about 75% informational / 25% literary.
- Informational contexts rotate among science, social studies/civics, workplace/community.
- MC items have four options.
- Editing practice should converge toward contextual embedded-choice editing, using workplace/community passages around 350–450 words.
- ER uses two source texts, up to about 650 words total, with a 45-minute analytical response scored across three traits.
- Close reading should remain central: key ideas, evidence, language, implied ideas, generalizations, perspective/purpose.

## Studo-specific decisions (not official GED rules)

- Use explicit question families to support transfer.
- Require distractor metadata and plain-language why-wrong rationales.
- Separate learning resources, practice, quiz, exam simulation, and spaced review.
- Use response distributions later for item revision and distractor quality checks.
