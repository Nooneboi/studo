## Phase 6B deliverable target

For each of the six skills:

- 1 Study Guide
- 2 Workbook Sheets
- 1 Interactive Practice (8 focused questions)

After the skill pages are populated, add 3 mixed Passage Practice sets that combine these skills naturally without duplicating the passage on each skill page.

## Production order

1. Sequence
2. Cause and effect
3. Compare / contrast
4. Inference
5. Conclusions
6. Relationships among people/events/ideas
7. Mixed passage sets
8. Full domain QA

Inference and Conclusions receive extra scrutiny because they depend heavily on evidence sufficiency and are easy to make ambiguous.
