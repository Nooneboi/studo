Study philosophy

Zoom out to plan.
Zoom in to master.

Every subject worth learning has three layers. Skip one and the others wobble. This is how the material on this site is organized — and how I'd suggest you study it.

Start practicing → Browse resources
▼ ▼
Plans

Where are you headed?

The broad shape: what you're trying to become able to do, and by when. Plans set direction, not detail — revisit them weekly, not daily.

Systems

What do you repeat?

The habits and routines that carry you toward the plan without relying on motivation: a phrase bank you review daily, a set schedule for passages, a fixed quiz routine.

Details

What are you practicing right now?

The individual question, the single word, the one passage in front of you. This is where the Practice page lives — the sharp end of the plan.

Why plans → systems → details?

A plan without a system stays a wish. A system without details to practice on has nothing to run on. Working top-down keeps you from drilling questions that don't serve a real direction — and working bottom-up, from real practice, is what tells you whether the plan needs adjusting.

How to use this site

Head to Practice for question sets — you can answer, change your mind, remove questions you've mastered, highlight anything tricky, and jot notes as you go. Everything you do there stays on your device. Check Resources for the phrase banks and reading that back the questions up.
