Methodology

How the learning material is built.

Different formats have different jobs

Study Guides teach the skill. Workbook Sheets make the learner apply it with reasoning. Interactive Practice gives immediate feedback and records local practice signals. Passage Practice combines several skills in one reading set.

Question development

Questions are planned around a declared skill, evidence path, reasoning demand, and plausible distractors. Difficulty should come from the reading and reasoning required, not from confusing wording.

Sources and originality

Official public educator materials are used to understand assessment targets and public item patterns. Chee Skool does not use secure test content. Learner passages are original, public-domain, licensed, or otherwise used only when the source and rights are documented.

What progress means

Chee Skool's skill percentages and review signals describe practice inside Chee Skool. They are not official GED® scores and are not a prediction of whether a learner will pass the official test.
