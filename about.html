About

An independent learning project.

Chee Skool is being built as a focused place to learn, practice, and review Reasoning Through Language Arts skills. It is not an official testing service and does not claim to reproduce secure exam questions.

What we are building

Each skill is designed to connect clear instruction, workbook practice, interactive practice, and mixed passage sets. The goal is useful preparation rather than a large pile of disconnected questions.

Independent status

Chee Skool is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by GED Testing Service LLC. GED® is a registered trademark of GED Testing Service LLC. References to the GED® test are used to describe the exam learners are preparing for.

Alpha status

Before public release, the project is being tested for content accuracy, usability, accessibility, source rights, navigation, and technical reliability.
